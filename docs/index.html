Privacy Policy — GAMA Uploader

Last updated: July 2026

GAMA Uploader ("the App") is a personal-use desktop application that helps its owner upload videos and set video thumbnails on their own YouTube channel(s) using the official YouTube Data API v3.

What data we access

When you authorize the App via Google Sign-In, it requests the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload videos and manage video metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status, and made-for-kids status) on the YouTube channel you authorize.

The App does not request access to your emails, contacts, files, or any Google data outside of the YouTube upload scope above.

How we use your data

Data obtained through this authorization is used solely to:

  • Upload video files that you explicitly select, to the YouTube channel you authorize.
  • Set the title, description, tags, thumbnail image, privacy status, and made-for-kids declaration that you provide for each video.

All actions are initiated directly by you, the App's owner and sole user, running the App on your own computer.

Data storage

OAuth authorization tokens are stored locally on your own computer, in the App's local data folder. They are never transmitted to any third-party server. The App does not have a backend server — all communication happens directly between your computer and Google's YouTube Data API.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data obtained through this authorization to any third party, for any purpose.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time via your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
